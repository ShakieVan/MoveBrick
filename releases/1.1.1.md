# Move, Brick 1.1.1 — up stays up

- **Turning keeps the figure upright:** dragging sideways turns the figure around its upright axis, dragging up or down tilts it towards you or away from you, almost to the view from straight above or below. The figure no longer rolls, so what is up in the figure is always up on the screen. Every side can still be reached.

Levels, options, helps and the updater are unchanged. The app's texts are in German.

## How to update

From 1.0.0 or 1.1.0: open **Updates** on the title screen, download and install. Details: [Installing and updating](https://github.com/ShakieVan/MoveBrick/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md).

## Google Play Protect

Version 1.0.0 was blocked by Play Protect after a requested scan on November 24, 2026 ("this app may be harmful", no reason named); VirusTotal reported 0 of 64 detections for that file, and an appeal was sent to Google. This version was not submitted to a Play Protect scan. Nothing here is a clearance; see [Google Play Protect](https://github.com/ShakieVan/MoveBrick/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#google-play-protect).

## Known limits

- Played on an Android 16 emulator only, with swipes sent by the test tool. Not checked on a phone.
- Sound, vibration and the two-finger gestures were not checked.
