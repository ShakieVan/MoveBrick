# Move, Brick 1.0.0 — first public version

*Get out the way!* A figure built of bricks floats in space, every brick carries an arrow. Tap a brick and it flies off along its arrow, if no other brick stands in its way. Clear them all.

- **Tap, turn, zoom:** drag to turn the figure, use two fingers to zoom and to shift it.
- **Endless computed levels,** from a handful of bricks to about 300. Every level can be solved.
- **No clock unless you want one:** "Auf Zeit spielen" (play against time) is an option and off at first. No limit of moves; stars for a nearly clean run.
- **Help:** a hint ("Tipp"), and once per level each: turn all arrows around ("Drehen"), clear five bricks ("Räumen"), shuffle the arrows ("Mischen").
- **Colour aid** ("Farbhilfe"): the colour of a brick shows its direction.
- **Updates from this page:** "Updates" on the title screen checks, downloads and installs a newer version when you ask it to.
- No advertising, no purchases, no account. The app's texts are in German.

## How to install

Download the APK below, open it and confirm Android's installer. Details: [Installing and updating](https://github.com/ShakieVan/MoveBrick/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md).

## Known limits

- Played on an Android 16 emulator; on a Galaxy S21 only the start, the first level and one move were looked at.
- Sound, vibration and the two-finger gestures were not checked before publication.
- In levels with wide, flat figures the outermost bricks can reach past the edge of the screen at first; zoom out with two fingers or turn the figure.
