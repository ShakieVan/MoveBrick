# Move, Brick 1.5.0 — German and English

- **Two languages:** the app now speaks German and English. At first the phone decides: German if German comes before English among the phone's languages, English otherwise.
- **Switchable:** the first entry in the options ("Sprache · Language" or "Language · Sprache") switches between **Deutsch** and **English** at once. The choice is kept, whatever language the phone is set to.
- In English the three helps are called **Flip** (turn all arrows around), **Clear** (take five bricks away) and **Mix** (deal the arrows anew); the kinds of level are **Solvable** and **Chance**.

Nothing else changes: levels, progress, saved levels and a running wait for a hint are kept.

## How to update

From any older version: open **Updates** on the title screen, download and install. Details: [Installing and updating](https://github.com/ShakieVan/MoveBrick/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md).

## Google Play Protect

Version 1.0.0 was blocked by Play Protect after a requested scan on November 24, 2026 ("this app may be harmful", no reason named); VirusTotal reported 0 of 64 detections for that file, and an appeal was sent to Google. This version was not submitted to a Play Protect scan. Nothing here is a clearance; see [Google Play Protect](https://github.com/ShakieVan/MoveBrick/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#google-play-protect).

## Known limits

- Played on an Android 16 emulator only. Not checked on a phone, and not on Android 9 to 15.
- Phones set to a language other than German or English show English, unless German is among their languages.
- Sound, vibration and the two-finger gestures were not checked.
