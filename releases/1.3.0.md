# Move, Brick 1.3.0 — concentration and Ultrahard

- **Concentration** ("Konzentration", from level 15 on): one wrong move blows the whole figure up, and the level starts again. The mistakes still count for the stars. A switch in the options turns it off; it is on at first.
- **Ultrahard,** a second mode with a row of levels of its own, on the title screen from the start:
  - Only very large figures, and they grow with every level, without end: about 280 cells in the first level, over 730 in level 114.
  - A wrong move always blows the figure up. There are no hints, no helps and no levels of chance.
  - It is locked until levels 1 to 20 are done, levels 15 to 20 with concentration switched on. The button tells which levels are still missing.
- **No shortcuts:** a wrong move is stored the moment it happens, so closing the app does not take it back. The Ultrahard record, what opened it and a level left half done carry a seal; if the stored progress was edited from outside, the Ultrahard record is reset. The seal keeps casual tampering out. It cannot stop someone who takes the app apart, and no record of an offline game can be proven.

The normal levels are unchanged, and a level left half done in 1.2.0 is continued. The app's texts are in German.

## How to update

From any older version: open **Updates** on the title screen, download and install. Details: [Installing and updating](https://github.com/ShakieVan/MoveBrick/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md).

This version was not submitted to a Play Protect scan. Nothing here is a clearance; see [Google Play Protect](https://github.com/ShakieVan/MoveBrick/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#google-play-protect).

## Known limits

- Played on an Android 16 emulator only. Not checked on a phone.
- Building a very late Ultrahard level takes a moment; how long on a phone was not measured.
- Sound (including the new explosion), vibration and the two-finger gestures were not checked.
