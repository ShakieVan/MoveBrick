# Move, Brick 1.5.1 — two fingers move the view

Made for the very large figures of Ultrahard, whose bricks are too small for a finger unless you zoom in.

- **Shifting works:** two fingers moving together take the figure along, once you have zoomed in. Up to 1.5.0 this only happened while the fingers were spreading or pinching at the same time, so in practice the view stayed on the middle of the figure.
- **Zoom where your fingers are:** spreading two fingers zooms in on the spot between them, not on the middle of the figure. The brick under your fingers stays under them.
- **Turning stays on the spot:** the figure turns around what is in the middle of the screen, so the part you zoomed in on stays in sight. Up is still always up.
- **Back to the overview:** zoom all the way out and the figure is in the middle again. The view cannot leave the figure.
- **A steady view:** zoomed in, the view stays on the same bricks while others fly off.
- **Hints:** a hint picks a brick that is on the screen; if none there can leave, the view glides to the brick it shows.

Nothing else changes: levels, progress, saved levels and a running wait for a hint are kept.

## How to update

From any older version: open **Updates** on the title screen, download and install. Details: [Installing and updating](https://github.com/ShakieVan/MoveBrick/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md).

## Known limits

- The gestures were played by a test tool that sends the movements of two fingers, not by a hand: with a test build on a Galaxy S10 (Android 16, LineageOS) on a figure of 155 bricks, in both views, and with the published file on an Android 16 emulator.
- Android itself takes two fingers for a zoom only while they are about 27 mm or more apart (its own setting, read on that phone and on the emulator). Closer together they shift the figure but do not zoom.
- Sound and vibration were not checked.
