# Move, Brick 1.4.0 — a fuse, a wait for hints, and perspective

- **A fuse before the bang** (concentration and Ultrahard): after a wrong move the brick that was hit glows from within for five seconds, pulsing faster and stronger, with a beep like a time bomb. The glow spreads to the bricks around it, so it also shows when the brick sits inside the figure. You can still turn the figure and see what happened. Then everything blows up and the level starts again. The mistake is stored the moment the fuse is lit.
- **Hints have to wait** ("Tipp"): after a hint the next one is available 15 minutes later in level 1, and a minute more with every level (34 minutes in level 20). Real time counts, also while the app is closed; the button shows what is left. The wait is measured by the time the phone has been running, so setting the clock does not shorten it; only after a restart of the phone the clock is used once.
- **Saved after every move:** the level you are in is stored after each move, not only when you leave. The normal levels and Ultrahard each keep a level of their own, and the title screen offers both with "Weiter". There is no save you can load at will: saving before every risky move would defeat concentration.
- **Perspective** ("Perspektive", an option, off at first): the usual view shows the figure through a long lens, with edges that run nearly parallel, much like an isometric drawing. Switched on, the view is a wide angle from close by: what is near is larger than what is far.

A level left half done in 1.3.0 is continued. The app's texts are in German.

## How to update

From any older version: open **Updates** on the title screen, download and install. Details: [Installing and updating](https://github.com/ShakieVan/MoveBrick/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md).

## Google Play Protect

Version 1.0.0 was blocked by Play Protect after a requested scan on November 24, 2026 ("this app may be harmful", no reason named); VirusTotal reported 0 of 64 detections for that file, and an appeal was sent to Google. This version was not submitted to a Play Protect scan. Nothing here is a clearance; see [Google Play Protect](https://github.com/ShakieVan/MoveBrick/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#google-play-protect).

## Known limits

- Played on an Android 16 emulator only. Not checked on a phone.
- Sound (including the new beeps), vibration and the two-finger gestures were not checked.
- The wait for a hint after a restart of the phone was checked with prepared data, not by restarting a device.
- The seal on the stored progress keeps casual tampering out. It cannot stop someone who takes the app apart.
