# Move, Brick 1.2.0 — large bricks, no gaps

- **Large bricks:** from level 10 on, some bricks are plates that cover four cells, with one big arrow. A large brick only flies off if nothing stands in its way across its whole width. They become more frequent as the levels go on, in both kinds of level ("Lösbar" and "Zufall").
- **No gaps:** bricks now touch each other; a dark line along their edges sets them apart. You can no longer peek between them into the figure.
- A brick that is tapped while it touches the one in its way flinches briefly instead of pushing into it.

Levels 1 to 9 are the same figures as before. From level 10 on the figures are cut into bricks differently, so a level left half done in an older version starts again from the beginning; open levels and stars are kept. The app's texts are in German.

## How to update

From any older version: open **Updates** on the title screen, download and install. Details: [Installing and updating](https://github.com/ShakieVan/MoveBrick/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md).

This version was not submitted to a Play Protect scan. Nothing here is a clearance; see [Google Play Protect](https://github.com/ShakieVan/MoveBrick/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md#google-play-protect).
