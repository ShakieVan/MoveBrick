# Move, Brick 1.1.0 — two kinds of level, signs optional

- **Difficulty** ("Schwierigkeit" in the options), two kinds of level:
  - **"Lösbar" (solvable),** as before: the arrows are arranged so that every figure can be cleared. Each help is there once per level.
  - **"Zufall" (chance),** new: the arrows are pure chance. Usually the figure gets stuck sooner or later. The app tells you when no brick can leave, and the helps "Drehen" (turn), "Räumen" (clear five) and "Mischen" (shuffle) have no limit.
- **Dot and cross optional** ("Punkt und Kreuz zeigen"): switch them off and only the arrows are drawn, which is harder to read.
- **Larger figures:** balls, diamonds, crosses and rings fill the screen better, and wide, flat figures no longer reach past the edge when a level starts.
- A level you leave keeps its kind and is continued as it was.

The figure of a level is the same in both kinds. The app's texts are in German.

## How to update

From 1.0.0: open **Updates** on the title screen, download and install. Details: [Installing and updating](https://github.com/ShakieVan/MoveBrick/blob/main/INSTALLATION.md).
